1. Introduction

Welcome to WordWise - Word Meaning Puzzle Game ("WordWise", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"). WordWise is a lightweight, offline-first educational application designed to help players expand their vocabulary, specifically targeting words from the Oxford 3000 and GRE databases, through engaging quizzes and flashcards.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be processed during your use of the App. This Privacy Policy outlines our data policies and practices. By installing and using WordWise, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. WordWise is built with your absolute privacy in mind.

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, transfer, or store personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device accounts.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: The App keeps track of gameplay statistics which are stored solely on your device. This includes your daily progress counters, daily goal settings, streak metrics, level configurations, and scores (coins balance). This data is completely isolated within your device container.

3. How We Use Data

Since any gameplay and progression metrics are stored locally, this data is processed exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To provide, personalize, and improve your local learning experience.
  • To track your study progress, learning streaks, and daily vocabulary goals inside the App interface.
  • To unlock difficulty tiers and stages in the game dynamically as you complete levels.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All game states, rewards, coins, streaks, and progress records are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences.

Important Note: Because all game progress is stored only on your local device repository, clearing the App's data or uninstalling WordWise will permanently delete your progression, coins, and current streak. We do not have any access to backup or restore this data.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

No third-party data sharing is performed.

WordWise does not contain any third-party ads, analytics SDKs, trackers, dynamic profiling, or social media integration plugins. Your learning metrics are strictly personal to you and do not leave your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe and suitable for all ages, including children. Because WordWise **does not knowingly or unknowingly collect, process, or transmit personal data of any kind** from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international guidelines protecting children's digital space under 13 years of age.

7. Security

Since no data is transmitted to or stored on external servers, there is no threat of cloud-based server database breaches. Security is maintained locally by the Android Operating System's application sandboxing mechanics, which prevents other applications on your device from accessing WordWise's local SharedPreferences file.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any modifications by posting the new Privacy Policy document on this web page. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting.
